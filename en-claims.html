How it works

From file to verified claim, on its own.

It connects to your accounts system or insurer monitor and works on the schedule you define: at night, on weekends or continuously.

1
Picks up the pending accountsFilters by insurer, status and dates
2
Reads the whole fileForm, invoice, charge detail, eligibility and prescription (automatic reading)
3
Applies your clinic’s matrixService type, amount, ICD-10/ICD-9 diagnosis, signatures, dates

Records and verifiesClaim number back in your system + audit

Features

Everything a good claims analyst would do.

Smart reading

Extracts data from scanned forms, invoices and prescriptions, even with handwriting on the prescription.

Assisted coding

Suggests the ICD-10 diagnosis and its ICD-9 equivalent from the form or prescription.

✍Document control

Detects a missing patient or doctor signature, stamp and ID number, and inconsistent dates.

Duplicate check

Cross-checks invoices and existing claims before submitting.

Robust search

Finds the insured across every combination of policy, certificate and name.

Post-submission check

Re-reads the portal and confirms the claim number of every operation.

Canary and alarms

If the portal changes or something does not add up, it stops before making mistakes.

Automatic schedule

Runs on its own on the programmed schedule, with a one-click start.

Audit and reports

Daily report: submitted, verified, for review and reasons.
