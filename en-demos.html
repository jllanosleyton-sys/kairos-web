          Kairos Docs

          Reads the document, extracts the fields, checks that the total adds up and loads it into your system. Pick an example or edit the text.

          What you have tried 0

            Kairos Assist

            Answers your team citing the company manual. If the manual does not cover it or the topic is sensitive, it does not make things up: it hands over to the responsible person.

              Kairos Inbox

              Classifies each email, answers the repetitive with approved templates and opens tickets. Complaints and anything ambiguous go to a person.

              What you have tried 0

                Kairos Voice

                Calls to confirm appointments with an approved script. Respects consent, schedule and a maximum of 3 attempts. Pick an appointment and answer the call yourself.
