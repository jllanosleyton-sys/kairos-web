Sheet 2 The method

From the first conversation to daily operation.

Four stages, no surprises. Tap each one to see an example of what you receive.

Conversation

We review a process with you and tell you, with your numbers, whether it is worth automating. If it is not, we tell you.

Proposal with your numbers

What you lose today, what you save and a demo of the process with sample data, without touching your real information. If the process needs more analysis: Kairos Assessment ($490, credited in full if you hire the implementation).

Supervised pilot

A single product, with a success criterion agreed in writing before we start. We review 100% of what it does and you decide whether we continue.

Month-to-month operation

Daily monitoring, adjustments and a monthly value report with the figures against the starting point. The following products in the package, with 20% off implementation.

Terms

Currency and payment

USD (balboas at par), excl. ITBMS tax. Transfer or ACH to the account shown on your invoice, business Yappy or card. Electronic invoice.

Payment schedule

Payment by milestones: 50% on signing and 50% when you accept the pilot with the measured results. Monthly fees in advance. 10% discount for annual payment.

Commitment

No lock-in: if the pilot does not meet the success criterion agreed in writing, you can end it without penalty. After the pilot everything is month to month. Timing for each case is confirmed in the written proposal.

Kairos Referral

Coming recommended by a client or partner? Your Kairos Assessment is free and gets 10% off implementation. If you are a client: one month of Kairos Care at no cost for each company you recommend that signs.
