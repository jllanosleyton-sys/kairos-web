1. Who we are

Kairos (“we”) is the trade name under which its founder, Jacobo Llanos Leyton, operates while the incorporation of Kairos Group, S.A. (in formation) in the Republic of Panama is completed. Once the company is registered it will assume the role of controller and we will update this policy. Location: Panama City. We are the data controller for the personal data collected through this website and our business relationships, in accordance with Law 81 of March 26, 2019 on Personal Data Protection and its regulations.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Kairos website, forms, commercial communications and the relationship with clients, prospects, suppliers and candidates. The processing of data we carry out on behalf of our clients within the contracted services is governed by the services contract and the corresponding Data Processing Agreement (DPA) (see section 5).

5. Our clients’ data within the services

Kairos principle: our clients’ sensitive data does not leave their infrastructure.
  • When we automate processes, the client is the controller and Kairos acts as processor, only under their documented instructions.
  • By default, bots run on the client’s equipment or cloud. Our code repositories contain no personal data: they are published through allow-lists and pass automatic privacy reviewers.
  • Kairos staff access only what is strictly necessary, under signed confidentiality. The reports we use for support contain counts, statuses and technical identifiers, not personal data.
  • In healthcare we also apply the patient confidentiality rules in force in Panama and the instructions of the healthcare facility.
  • When the service ends, we return or delete the data as the contract provides and deliver written confirmation.

6. Use of artificial intelligence

  • We do not use client data to train our own or third-party AI models.
  • We only send information to external AI services with the client’s express authorization, to providers that do not retain or train on that data and, where possible, after anonymizing it.
  • Automated decisions that affect people always have human review available. See our Responsible AI Policy.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with:

  • Providers that deliver services to us (email, web hosting, electronic invoicing, accounting), under confidentiality contracts.
  • Google (Google Calendar and Google Meet), when you book a conversation in our online calendar: it receives your name, email and whatever you write in the booking form.
  • Meta (WhatsApp), when you write to us on WhatsApp or submit the contact form, which opens WhatsApp with your message.
  • Legal advisors and auditors.
  • Competent authorities, when the law requires it.

8. International transfers

Some technology providers (for example, email or hosting) may be located outside Panama. In those cases we require equivalent protection safeguards, in accordance with Law 81 of 2019.

10. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures: encryption in transit, least-privilege access control, multi-factor authentication, credentials in secure managers, audit logs, automatic privacy reviews of code and an incident response plan. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify it as the law requires. More details in Security.

11. Your rights

You may exercise at any time your rights of Access, Rectification, Cancellation, Opposition and Portability, and withdraw your consent. Write to privacidad@kairosgrouppanama.com stating your name, the right you are exercising and a way to contact you. We respond within the legal deadline. If you are not satisfied, you may go to the National Authority for Transparency and Access to Information (ANTAI).

12. Cookies

This site does not use cookies. Typefaces are hosted on the site itself, so your browser does not connect to third parties to display them. We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify anyone: it only gives aggregate figures (for example, how many people viewed a page). If you arrive from one of our posts or ads, the link indicates its origin (for example, “linkedin”); we keep it only in that tab of your browser, without cookies, and it is sent to us only if you decide to write to us on WhatsApp or submit the form. To do this your browser loads a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com, which receives technical data about the visit (page, browser, approximate country); Cloudflare does not use it to build profiles or for advertising. External services you choose to visit (online calendar, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram) have their own policies.

13. Minors

Our services are aimed at companies. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

14. Changes to this policy

We will publish any change here and indicate the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will notify our clients by email.
