Do you see my patients’ data?

Not in the Express Assessment: only volumes and approximate times. At implementation, the bot runs on your infrastructure, with an NDA and a data processing agreement signed before any access.

What happens if the insurer’s portal changes?

Kairos Care monitors the bot every day and adjusts to portal changes; if something does not add up, the case goes to a person on your team.

Does it work with my insurer?

It was tested in a real pilot with one insurer; each new insurer is configured and tested in a pilot before going live.

What if a claim has no signature or diagnosis?
