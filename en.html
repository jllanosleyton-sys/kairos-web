    Guarantees

    Three rules we never bend.

    1. 1First,

      your rules

      We write every rule of the process with your team. The system does nothing you have not approved.

      How you can checkYou sign the list of rules before we start.

    2. 2Always,

      a person

      When in doubt, the system does not decide: it sets the case aside, notes why and hands it to someone on your team.

      How you can checkEvery case set aside stays in the log, with who resolved it.

    3. 3Never,

      your data outside

      Everything runs inside your company. Your data is not used for anything else.

      How you can checkConfidentiality and data processing (Panama Law 81 of 2019) signed before we start.
