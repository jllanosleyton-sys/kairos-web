The method

Four problems, one method.

Almost all repetitive work in a company falls into one of these four families. Find yours: how it looks today and how it looks when it runs itself.

  • Customer service

    Emails and messages
    Today
    Messages that wait for hours or get lost at night and on weekends.
    With Kairos
    Every message is answered or routed to the right person, with templates you approved.

    For exampleA quote requested at 11 p.m. is answered with your template.

    SolutionsInboxAssistCRM

  • Scheduling

    Appointments and reminders
    Today
    Hours on the phone and WhatsApp confirming and moving appointments.
    With Kairos
    Appointments are confirmed, moved and reminded on their own, with your real schedule.

    For exampleA client asks to move an appointment on a Sunday and it’s rescheduled.

    SolutionsAgendaVoice

  • Collections

    Invoices and claims
    Today
    Overdue invoices nobody chases and claims that come back over a detail.
    With Kairos
    Every invoice gets its reminder on time and every claim goes out complete.

    For exampleAn overdue invoice gets its notice on time and the payment promise is logged.

    SolutionsCobraClaimsMonitor

  • Documents

    Read, compare and load
    Today
    Someone copies invoices, orders and files by hand; the error shows up after it’s paid.
    With Kairos
    Each document is read automatically, compared against your system and loaded. If a signature is missing or a total doesn’t match, it stops.

    For exampleA supplier invoice is compared with its purchase order before it’s paid.

    SolutionsDocsDocCheck

Three non-negotiable rules

  • Your rules first

    The system does nothing you have not approved in writing.

  • Your data stays in

    Everything runs inside your company, with signed confidentiality (Law 81 of 2019).
