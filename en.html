FAQ

What clients usually ask.

What if the pilot doesn't work?

We agree on a success criterion in writing before starting. If the 30-day pilot doesn't meet it, you end it with no penalty and pay only the initial 50% of the implementation.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. After the pilot everything is month to month, cancellable with 30 days' notice and no penalty.

Does the bot decide on its own?

Only within the rules you approve. Every doubtful case goes to a person on your team, and every action is logged and verified.

Do I need to change my systems?

No. Kairos works on the systems and portals you already use, the way an analyst on your team would.

How much does it cost to start?

The 30-minute Express Assessment is free. The full Kairos Assessment is US$490, fully credited if you hire the implementation within 60 days. See our price list (Spanish).

Which products are ready today?

Kairos Claims completed a real pilot with one insurer. Agenda and Monitor have live demos. The rest are in development and built on demand.
